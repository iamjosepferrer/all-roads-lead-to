How this was made

Roads come from OpenStreetMap (via the Overture Maps October 2026 release): motorways, trunk and primary roads plus ferry routes, across the EU, the UK, EFTA and the Western Balkans. Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, Russia and Turkey are left out.

Speeds use the signed limit where it exists, otherwise 120, 100 and 80 km/h by road class and 60 on slip roads. Ferries run at 35 km/h with 20 minutes to board at each end; the Channel Tunnel shuttle takes 65 minutes and stays on in Roads only, since it is a train. No traffic, tolls or turn costs, and one-way rules are ignored.

Every road segment carries the people who live closest to it (GHSL 2025 population grid, 1 km). Line width is everyone whose fastest route passes along that road, so it grows towards the destination.

Secondary and local roads are not in this version, so short gaps between main roads are bridged with straight links at 40 km/h. Routing runs in your browser.

Idea from Todos los caminos llevan a… (Spain) and Arterial Mapping's All Roads Lead to Richmond.
